## Operating standards

- No guaranteed sales, rankings, reach, income or conversion results.
- Customers approve factual claims, prices, testimonials and credentials.
- Nothing is published without owner approval.
- No fabricated testimonials, urgency, scarcity or relationships.
- No bulk spam or reuse of customer data outside the authorized job.
- Regulated financial, health, housing, employment and legal advertising requires additional review.

## Revenue and research policy

Service revenue is recorded before allocation. Only cleared revenue remaining after fees, refunds, taxes set aside and direct delivery costs counts as verified net profit.

Trading bots are a separate high-risk research lane. After at least $300 in verified net service profit, the owner may separately approve up to $100 for paper trading and historical testing. Live orders, leverage, borrowed money, automatic transfers and automatic loss replenishment are prohibited without explicit owner authorization. No profit is guaranteed.
